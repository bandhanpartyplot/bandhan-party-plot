Our Journey

A Story Born From a Passion for Celebrations


Bandhan Party Plot was founded in 2013 with a simple yet powerful vision — to create a space where Gujarat's families could celebrate life's most precious moments in absolute grandeur and comfort.

What began as a single banquet hall has grown into a sprawling 30,000 sq. ft. celebration complex, featuring multiple event spaces, a lush garden venue, and state-of-the-art facilities. Over the past decade, we've had the honour of hosting more than 500 events, each one uniquely special.

Our founder, Shri Rameshbhai Patel, believed that every family deserves a venue that treats their occasion with the same care and dedication as a wedding in the finest hotels — but at a price that respects their hard-earned budget. That philosophy remains our guiding principle to this day.

2013 — The Beginning

Bandhan Party Plot opens its doors with a single hall and a team of 10 dedicated staff members.

2016 — Garden Venue Added

We expanded with a beautiful open-air garden space, perfect for evening celebrations under the stars.

2019 — Premium Renovation

A complete interior makeover brought chandeliers, marble flooring, and a royal bridal suite.

2022 — 500th Event Milestone

We celebrated hosting our 500th event — a landmark we're immensely proud of.

2024 — Digital Transformation

Launched our online booking system and dedicated event management app for clients.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Bandhan Party Plot

Our main banquet hall can accommodate up to 1,500 guests for seated dining arrangements. For cocktail-style events, the capacity can be extended to 2,000 guests. Our garden venue can host up to 800 guests for open-air celebrations.

We recommend booking at least 3–6 months in advance, especially for wedding dates and peak seasons (October–February). For smaller events like birthdays, 1–2 months advance booking is usually sufficient. Popular dates can get booked quickly!

Yes! We offer complete in-house catering with a team of experienced chefs. Our menu includes authentic Gujarati cuisine, North Indian, South Indian, Chinese, and continental options. We also accommodate special dietary requirements including Jain and vegan menus. Outside catering is also permitted with prior permission.

Absolutely! Bandhan Party Plot offers free, dedicated parking space for over 500 vehicles. The parking area is well-lit, secured, and managed by our trained parking attendants. Valet parking service is also available upon request for premium events.
