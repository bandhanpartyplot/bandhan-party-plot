Everything You Need for a Perfect Celebration

From the moment you walk in to the moment the last guest leaves — we handle everything

🏛️

Banquet Hall & Venue

Our premium air-conditioned banquet hall is the centerpiece of Bandhan Party Plot, designed to impress and accommodate every type of grand event.

  • 30,000 sq. ft. total event space
  • Capacity up to 1,500 seated guests
  • Marble flooring & crystal chandeliers
  • Grand entrance archway & stage
  • Private bridal suite & groom's room
  • Separate green rooms for artists
🍽️

Premium Catering

Our culinary team brings decades of experience crafting multi-course feasts that delight every palate. Fresh ingredients, authentic recipes, and impeccable presentation.

  • Authentic Gujarati cuisine specialists
  • North Indian, South Indian & Chinese
  • Continental & fusion options
  • Jain, vegan & dietary-specific menus
  • Live cooking stations (Chaat, Ice cream)
  • Unlimited servings during event
🎨

Decoration & Themes

Our in-house decoration team transforms any space into a visual masterpiece. From traditional mandap setups to contemporary designer themes — we bring your vision to life.

  • Traditional & royal mandap setups
  • Floral paradise decoration
  • Custom theme design & execution
  • LED & fairy light installations
  • Balloon & flower archways
  • Photo booths & backdrop setups
🎤

Sound, Lighting & DJ

Professional-grade audio-visual systems ensure every note is perfect and every moment is beautifully lit. Our technical team is on-site for the entire duration of your event.

  • Professional surround sound system
  • Stage LED screen & projectors
  • Dynamic lighting & laser shows
  • In-house DJ with premium setups
  • Live band accommodation
  • Background music systems
🌿

Garden Venue

Celebrate under the open sky in our beautifully landscaped garden space — perfect for pre-wedding functions, cocktail evenings, and intimate family gatherings.

  • 10,000 sq. ft. lush garden area
  • Capacity up to 800 guests
  • Beautiful landscape and water features
  • Outdoor stage & tent facilities
  • Perfect for Haldi, Mehendi & Sangeet
  • Ambient outdoor lighting
👨‍💼

Event Management

Your dedicated event coordinator is your single point of contact from booking to the event day. They oversee every detail so you can simply enjoy your celebration.

  • Personal event coordinator assigned
  • Pre-event planning & consultations
  • Vendor coordination & management
  • On-day timeline management
  • Guest management & seating
  • Post-event cleanup & assistance
