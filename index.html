About Bandhan Party Plot

A Legacy of Grand Celebrations in Gujarat


Nestled in the heart of Gujarat, Bandhan Party Plot has been the destination of choice for families who seek nothing but the finest in celebration. For over a decade, we have crafted unforgettable experiences that blend tradition with modern luxury.

Our sprawling venue spans over 30,000 sq. ft., offering breathtaking banquet halls, lush garden spaces, and world-class amenities. Every event at Bandhan is treated as a unique story — your story — and our dedicated team ensures every chapter is perfect.
