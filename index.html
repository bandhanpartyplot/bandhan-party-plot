About Bandhan Party Plot

A Spacious, Comfortable Venue in the Heart of Kalol

Bandhan Party Plot is a spacious and comfortable event venue located in Kalol, Gujarat. We provide a welcoming environment for weddings, family functions, birthday parties, receptions, and special occasions, with convenient accessibility and dependable facilities.

We're located beside Sopan 2 Flat in Janta Nagar, Kalol — easy to find, easy to reach, and ready to host your celebration exactly the way you plan it.

  • Wheelchair-accessible venue
  • Free parking lot
  • Restroom facilities
  • Central Kalol location
